Why partner with us

Built for the agents
who send students to Japan

Several schools, one partner

Our group includes Japanese language schools and vocational colleges. Work with one contact and match each student with the right school.

A clear path in Japan

Students can move from a Japanese language school to a vocational college or university, and on to a job — with career support from CCC, our group's licensed job placement company.

Online applications in Japanese and English

Students apply through Compass, our own application system, in Japanese or English — no paper forms to send back and forth.

Every application tracked by agent

Compass records which agent each application came from, so we can give you clear, reliable status updates for your students.

Experienced with the visa process

Our schools prepare the Certificate of Eligibility application with Japan's Immigration Services Agency for admitted students coming from abroad.

Partnership terms by agreement

Partnership terms are agreed individually with each agent. Contact us and we will walk you through them.
