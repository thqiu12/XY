Help us build the
infrastructure of education.

XY, K.K. is a small team in Tokyo, Japan, building systems and AI products for educational institutions. We want to keep the speed and autonomy of a startup as we move to our next stage, and we are looking for people and partners to grow our products with us. Besides full-time roles, we welcome side-job, contract and advisory arrangements.

Why work at XY

What you build gets used in real schools.

Real problems from inside schools

You will work on problems that come from actually running educational institutions. These are products people really use every day, not ideas on paper.

Ownership and fast decisions

In a small team, your proposals turn directly into product. You work close to the people who decide and the people who build, and each person's work has wide impact.

Hands-on AI product work

You will build LLMs, RAG and AI agents into production products, and gain experience turning new technology into real value for schools.

Open positions

Who we're looking for.

For every role, we are flexible about the arrangement — full-time, side job or contract — and decide it together. If you're not sure whether you fit, feel free to get in touch.
