Our work

The jobs across the group

Japanese-language teacher

Teach international students at SGG Language School, full-time or part-time. Academic coordinator roles are also open.

School administration

Student records, attendance, grades and residence-status (COE) applications at the core of school operations.

Vocational school instructor

Teach university and graduate-school entrance preparation (math, physics, biology) at 神奈川柔整鍼灸専門学校 (Sagamihara, Kanagawa).

Head office

Labor, accounting and general affairs for the group's schools and companies.

Technology

Build software and AI products for educational institutions at XY, K.K.

Build the infrastructure of education with us.

XY is a small team building software and AI products for educational institutions. We work on problems that come from running schools ourselves, and what we build is used every day at the group's schools. Full-time, side-project, contract and advisory roles are all welcome: full-stack engineers, AI/LLM engineers, customer success, and advisors/partners.
