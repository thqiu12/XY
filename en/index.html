The problem

Does your school run on
a few key people?

At many educational institutions, recruitment, applications, student support and HR still run on paper, spreadsheets and the experience of individual staff. That work is hard to hand over, and it is where mistakes and overtime pile up.

Recruitment that depends on individualsInformation from first inquiry to enrollment is scattered, so follow-ups get missed or duplicated.
Repetitive paperworkRosters, document checks and reminder emails take up time staff could spend with students.
Data that never reaches managementWithout a clear view of recruitment and student activity, decisions fall back on gut feeling.

Why

Why technology, in education?

Education is work done by people. That is exactly why we use technology — to give people room for the work only people can do.

Recruitment, applications, enrollment records: much of a school's time disappears into paper, spreadsheets and repetitive administration. Having run schools ourselves, we know how much that costs. So we don't stop at advice from the outside. We build the systems ourselves and stay until they work. Giving teachers and staff back the time to face their students — that is why we founded XY.

Message from the CEO

Solving the problems I saw inside schools.

I was born in China, graduated from Keio University and completed a master's program at the University of Tokyo. Since 2012 I have worked in study-in-Japan services, advising students on undergraduate and graduate admissions and launching and running Japanese-language education businesses.

At Recruit Co., Ltd. I worked in corporate sales and web marketing before moving into business planning and new business development. I then joined the management of educational institutions and have run several education businesses, including Japanese language schools and vocational colleges.

Today I help lead an education group that welcomes more than 4,000 international students a year, and I serve as chair of an educational corporation and as representative of several education-related companies. Years of this work showed me how much schools struggle with complex administration and with operations that depend on a few individuals. I founded XY to solve that.

I personally lead our product development, using AI and a modern technology stack. We build our systems in-house with a small team and AI-assisted development, starting with Compass, our student application and management system. Our work spans workflow automation, knowledge retrieval with RAG, LLM tuning and on-premise AI, putting AI agents to work in real operations. We bring education and technology together from both the management and the engineering side.

Our mission is to raise the productivity of educational institutions and support their long-term growth through education and technology.
