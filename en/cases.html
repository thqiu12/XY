Compass, our student application
and management system, at work.

Compass, the student application and management system we build, runs in daily operations at schools operated within our education group — Haba Gakuen, Hirai Gakuen and SGG Gaigo Gakuin. It brings student recruitment, applications and student records into one place, reducing the administrative load on staff and moving school operations off paper.

In production

An application portal in daily use.

Applicants complete each step online, and school staff see the status of every application in one list. Information that used to be spread across paper and spreadsheets is in one place, so follow-ups are not missed or sent twice.
