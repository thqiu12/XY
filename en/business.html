Our business

Japanese language schools, vocational colleges, career support and the technology behind them, connecting every step from study to work in Japan.

Language school

Japanese language schools

SGG Gaigo Gakuin (Tokyo and Osaka) and Chinichi (Takadanobaba, Tokyo) run our Japanese language schools. Chinichi Co., Ltd. also supports the recruitment and education of international students.

SGG Language School website

Vocational college

Vocational colleges

Haba Gakuen and Hirai Gakuen run vocational colleges that support international students' studies.

Career support

Career support

Support after graduation: CCC Co., Ltd. is licensed in Japan for worker dispatch and job placement, and helps international students find work.

Technology

Technology
