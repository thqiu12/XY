Running a school corporation
on one platform.

chinichi-OS is an operating platform for school corporations that brings the functions needed to run schools onto a single foundation. Starting from applications, it connects student management, HR, enrollment and accounting, with the goal of becoming education infrastructure where people and AI work together. It is developed and rolled out by XY, K.K.

Concept

The idea of an operating platform for school corporations

Applications, student management, HR, enrollment and accounting. Rather than splitting school operations across separate tools, we bring them together on one platform. That is the idea behind chinichi-OS.

At many educational institutions, each task has its own tool or spreadsheet, the same information is entered again and again, and data ends up fragmented. chinichi-OS connects everything from the first application through enrollment and graduation in a single flow, removes double entry for staff, and turns operational records into data management can use. AI handles routine work in the background, so people can focus on the decisions only they should make.

Modules

Five connected areas, from applications to accounting.

Each area is a module that can be adopted in phases. The applications module (Compass) is live today, and the other modules are rolling out in turn.

Planned

Student management, HR, enrollment and accounting

Enrollment records, grades and attendance; time records and contracts for teachers and staff; residence status and status changes; tuition and payments. Starting from the data collected at the application stage, we are extending the platform across school operations.

Why integrated

Why integration?

The more tools you add, the more your information fragments and the more time goes into entering and checking it. By bringing everything onto one platform, chinichi-OS reverses that.

No more fragmented data

There is no need to enter the same information into several tools, which prevents transcription errors and conflicting records.

End-to-end operations

Application → admission → enrollment → graduation, managed as one flow. Information stays intact when staff or departments change.

Visibility for management

Recruitment, enrollment and accounting can be viewed side by side, so decisions are based on data rather than gut feeling.

Phased adoption

There is no need to replace everything at once. Start with the modules you need and expand as your operations allow.

Principles

Design principles

Built from inside schools

Led by a CEO who chairs an educational corporation and helps run an education group, we design around the work schools actually need done.

Non-disruptive, phased rollout

We put a priority on never stopping existing operations. Start small and expand as you confirm the results.

People and AI working together

AI supports routine work such as document checks and messaging, so people can focus on judgment and conversations.

Security first

In addition to operational safeguards such as encrypted backups, we support local (on-premise) AI configurations that keep confidential data inside your environment.

Vision

Human × AI × Global

chinichi-OS aims to be education infrastructure where people and AI work together. Starting from applications, it connects the whole of school operations and supports higher productivity and sustainable growth for educational institutions. Over time, we plan to develop it into a platform that more educational institutions can use.
